## Names we use descriptively

### "opencode"

"opencode" is a trademark of its respective owner. We use it descriptively, to
identify the software that this project connects to. We do not claim any
affiliation with, or endorsement by, the opencode project or its maintainers.

opencode-rc is a **remote control for opencode**, in the same way that a remote
control is a remote control for a television. The trademark nominative use here
identifies the target software and nothing more.

contributors, under the repository's MIT licence. You may use the name to refer
to the project. You may **not** use it in a way that implies your own product is

---

## If you build a fork

You may fork this project and rename it. Please do:

- remove references to the opencode-rc maintainers from `GOVERNANCE.md`,
  `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` and `SECURITY.md`, so nobody is contacted about software
  they do not maintain;
- keep the attribution in `NOTICE` about what opencode-rc was inspired by;
- keep the descriptive use of third-party trademarks accurate for *your*
  project.

Keeping the provenance accurate matters more than keeping the name. Anyone
building on this deserves to know the design came from somewhere specific.

---

## Reporting a trademark concern

If you believe something here misuses your mark, open an issue on the repository
or contact the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). We would
rather fix a description than argue about it.
